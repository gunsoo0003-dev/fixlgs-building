Applied decisions
1. TERMS

3. WHY PRICE IS ANALYZED THIS WAY

   - Seven blocks follow: user question → strong answer → easy explanation → professional concept → takeaway → next question.

   - Internal 'engine' wording is not exposed in user-facing copy.
   - Content depth retained while reducing cognitive load.

4. ANALYSIS METHOD
   - Seven-step practical method retained.
   - Each step includes what to check, why it matters, and cautions where needed.

5. REAL CASE
   - Concrete 15억원 case retained.
   - Comps: 14.2억 / 15.1억 / 13.8억.
   - Income cross-check: 6.4% headline → approx. 4.7% after vacancy/owner operating cost.
   - Interpretation expanded so users can follow how evidence leads to '가격 측면에서는 다소 불리합니다.'
   - Price-axis conclusion explicitly kept separate from whole-building buy/sell conclusion.

6. RECAP
   - Added a typography flow recap before tools.
   - PRICE analysis sequence is shown as seven connected decisions.
   - Final takeaway: price analysis verifies the evidence explaining a price; it does not merely guess one correct number.

7. RELATED TOOLS

   - 적정가격 비교 / 평당가 계산 retained.

Design principle
- High information density, low cognitive load.
- Large questions, strong short answers, comfortable body copy, selective blue emphasis, generous whitespace.
- No table/card wall for the reasoning section.
- Typography itself carries the reasoning flow.
